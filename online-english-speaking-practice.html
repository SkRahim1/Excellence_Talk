The 70-30 Speaking Ratio in Online Sessions

Traditional online tuition fails because teachers lecture for 55 minutes while 30 muted students stare at passive slides. At Excellence Talk, our virtual learning model reverses this completely: trainers speak only 30% of the time, while students speak 70% of the time in structured breakout debates, situational roleplays, and speed-speaking relays.

Outside of live classes, students log into the Excellence Talk Student Portal to complete daily 10-minute AI conversation missions synced directly to their grade-wise curriculum.
