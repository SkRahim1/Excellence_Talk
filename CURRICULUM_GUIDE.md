# Excellence Talk: Grade-Wise Curriculum & Phygital Mapping Guide

This document establishes the curriculum architecture, chapter blueprints, and physical-to-digital mapping matrix for the Excellence Talk learning system.

---

## 1. Grade-Wise Level Matrix

### Level 1: Early Explorers (Grades 1–2)
- **Primary Goal**: Spoken confidence, phonemic awareness, vocabulary building, and basic functional communication.
- **Printed Book Focus**:
  - Full-color illustrated phonics cards, sound blends, visual dictionaries.
  - Tracing and simple word/sentence filling exercises.
  - Rhymes, chants, and paired puppet/action dialogues.
- **Classroom Smart TV Mode**:
  - Sound tap & echo games (e.g., tap the screen to hear sound blends).
  - Whole-class sing-alongs with bouncing subtitles.
  - Picture flashcard team races.
- **AI Speaking Coach**:
  - Voice-activated animal, object, and color identification.
  - Simple 1-word to 3-word sentence mimicry with visual stickers as rewards.
- **Live Trainer Classes**:
  - Show & Tell sessions ("Bring your favorite toy to class").
  - Expressive read-alouds and emotional expression games.

---

### Level 2: Confident Communicators (Grades 3–5)
- **Primary Goal**: Sentence syntax, storytelling, situational dialogues, and self-expression.
- **Printed Book Focus**:
  - Chapter themes: *Daily Routines, My Neighborhood, The Science Fair, At the Market, Superheroes & Helpers*.
  - Sentence frames (e.g., "First, I... Next, I... Finally, I...").
  - Mini comic strip dialogues with speech bubbles to complete.
  - Peer roleplay cards.
- **Classroom Smart TV Mode**:
  - Interactive dialogue brancher (students vote on what character says next).
  - Sentence scramble relay on the interactive board.
  - Timed picture description buzzer game.
- **AI Speaking Coach**:
  - Guided conversational scenarios (e.g., "Tell the AI about your weekend morning routine").
  - Immediate feedback on grammar (past vs. present tense) and sentence clarity.
- **Live Trainer Classes**:
  - "News Anchor for a Day" live broadcasts.
  - Interactive story building (Trainer starts a story; each student adds a line).

---

### Level 3: Fluent Speakers (Grades 6–8)
- **Primary Goal**: Spontaneous conversational fluency, contextual grammar, structured opinions, and clear enunciation.
- **Printed Book Focus**:
  - Chapter themes: *Environmental Dilemmas, Cultural Festivals, Inventions of Tomorrow, Healthy Living, Overcoming Challenges*.
  - Idioms, phrasal verbs, and discourse markers ("In my perspective...", "On the contrary...").
  - Reading passages followed by structured analytical speaking prompts.
- **Classroom Smart TV Mode**:
  - Live opinion polling with instant screen debate topics.
  - Video comprehension stop-and-talk prompts.
  - Rapid-fire vocabulary quizzes with team leaderboards.
- **AI Speaking Coach**:
  - Spontaneous 60-second speech prompts with AI follow-up questions.
  - Analysis of pause frequencies, filler word count ("um", "like"), and lexical density.
- **Live Trainer Classes**:
  - Parliamentary-style mini-debates (Proposition vs. Opposition).
  - Book/Movie review presentations with peer critique rubrics.

---

### Level 4: Persuasive Leaders (Grades 9–10)
- **Primary Goal**: Rhetorical devices, public speaking, structured argumentation, and introductory interview acumen.
- **Printed Book Focus**:
  - Chapter themes: *Global Ethics, Digital Citizenship, Leadership Case Studies, Art of Negotiation, Career Horizons*.
  - Speech structure blueprints: Hook, Problem, Solution, Call to Action (Monroe's Motivated Sequence).
  - Formal vs. informal language register shifts.
- **Classroom Smart TV Mode**:
  - Deconstruction of famous historical speeches with annotated subtitles.
  - Impromptu "Balloon Debate" rounds run by the interactive timer.
- **AI Speaking Coach**:
  - Adversarial AI debate simulator (the AI challenges the student's argument with counter-arguments).
  - Formal interview simulator: Answering common behavioral questions.
- **Live Trainer Classes**:
  - Formal Model United Nations (MUN) style simulations.
  - High-impact elocution and impromptu speech contests.

---

### Level 5: Global Professionals (College / Higher Ed)
- **Primary Goal**: Corporate employability, high-stakes group discussions (GD), technical/HR interviews, and executive pitching.
- **Printed Book Focus (Professional English & Employability Workbook)**:
  - Modules: *Professional Identity & Personal Branding, The Art of the Group Discussion (Initiating, Moderating, Concluding), Corporate Email & Memo Writing, Cross-Cultural Business Etiquette, Technical & Behavioral Interview Mastery*.
  - Case study briefs with business dilemmas and data charts to present.
- **Classroom / Smart Lab Mode**:
  - Multi-participant boardroom simulation timer and rubric tracker.
  - Corporate crisis communication drills.
- **AI Speaking Coach**:
  - AI Mock HR & Technical Interviewer tailored by industry (IT, Finance, Healthcare, Engineering).
  - Pitch timer analyzing vocal variety, pitch modulation, and confidence index.
- **Live Trainer Sessions**:
  - Live 8-person Group Discussions moderated by corporate HR trainers with individual feedback scorecards.
  - 1-on-1 mock panel interviews with resume deconstruction.

## 2. Standard 45-Minute Lesson Architecture (Teacher Guide)

Every chapter in the printed book maps to this reproducible 45-minute lesson blueprint:

```
[00:00 - 00:08] 1. Hook & Warm-Up (Classroom Smart TV)
- Animated teaser or controversial question projected on screen.
- Fast class-wide vocabulary activation (3 mins).

[00:08 - 00:20] 2. Concept & Book Study (Printed Book)
- Students open Book Chapter; teacher guides reading of dialogue and sentence frame.
- Students write answers to Activity 1 & 2 in physical workbooks.

[00:20 - 00:32] 3. Interactive Class Drill (Classroom Smart TV)
- Whole-group interactive team game / paired roleplay exercise using screen visual prompts.
- Teacher circulates using presenter notes to assess struggling students.

[00:32 - 00:40] 4. Synthesis & Micro-Presentations
- 3-4 pairs present their dialogue to the class or complete an extempore drill.
- Class awards peer feedback tokens.

[00:40 - 00:45] 5. Wrap-Up & Digital Bridge Assignment
- Teacher points out the Chapter QR Code on the bottom of the book page.
- Homework assigned: Scan QR code -> Complete 5-minute AI Speaking Session before next class.
```

| Component | Cost Category | Typical Allocation | Notes |
