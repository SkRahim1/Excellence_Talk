Placement Reality

Why Technical Knowledge Alone Is No Longer Enough for Top Placements

Every year, thousands of talented engineering, management, and degree graduates clear coding and aptitude rounds, only to be rejected in the first 5 minutes of Group Discussions or Personal Interviews due to hesitant English, mother-tongue influence, or lack of structured argumentation.

Excellence Talk partners with universities, polytechnics, and degree colleges to deliver intensive, activity-based English communication bootcamps and semester-long speaking laboratories.

4 Placement-Ready Communication Modules

  • Group Discussion (GD) Mastery: Framing persuasive opening points, tactful counter-arguments, synthesising chaotic debates, and maintaining assertive body language.
  • Personal Interview Simulation: Formulating impactful answers to "Tell me about yourself", situational behavioural questions, and salary/role negotiations.
  • Executive Presentation Skills: Pitching technical project dissertations with structured narrative arcs and confident slide delivery.
  • Corporate Email & Etiquette: Drafting succinct professional emails, cross-functional meeting participation, and business telephone protocols.
