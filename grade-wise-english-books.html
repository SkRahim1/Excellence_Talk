Grade-Wise English Learning Books & Student Workbooks

Tactile, beautifully illustrated physical curriculum books designed to eliminate screen fatigue and build spontaneous spoken English fluency through daily classroom activities and QR-synced audio practice.

Pedagogical Design

Why Physical Books Are Essential for Spoken Language Mastery

While screen-only language apps suffer from high abandonment rates and digital distraction, tactile printed books provide deep cognitive absorption. Students annotate, trace sentence patterns, read dialogues aloud, and collaborate side-by-side with desk partners.

Every Excellence Talk workbook is mapped to internationally recognised CEFR (Common European Framework of Reference for Languages) speaking benchmarks while reflecting vibrant, relatable Indian school contexts.

What Makes Every Chapter Unique?

  • Comic Strip Situations: Relatable conversational scenarios (e.g. asking a librarian, resolving a playground dispute, ordering snacks).
  • Pattern Practice Frames: Structured sentence frames that stop mental translation and train students to think directly in English.
  • 1-to-1 Chapter QR Codes: Scan to listen to authentic native pronunciation models and launch AI speech evaluations.
  • Classroom Activity Prompts: Rapid-fire team relays, roleplay scripts, and debates requiring zero teacher preparation time.

Curriculum Grade Tiers

Grade 1–2: Talk & Wonder

Phonics, sight words, conversational action chants, picture talk.

Grade 3–5: Talk & Tell

Conversational storytelling, paired dialogues, functional grammar.

Grade 6–8: Talk & Connect

Spontaneous public speaking, contextual vocabulary, situational roleplays.

Grade 9–10: Talk & Lead

Elocution, formal debates, Model UN, group discussion leadership.
